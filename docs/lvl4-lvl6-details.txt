The prompt explained in the video is provided here.
The moon swells past half, brightening fast. Your MVP goes live on Preprod with documentation, CI/CD, and a public product profile on X. More is disclosed now than hidden — the shape of a real product is impossible to mistake.
Your mission this cycle: MVP live on Preprod, with docs, CI/CD, and a public product (X) profile.
Who Can Join?
Open to developers who have completed Level 3 with an approved idea, ready to build a real MVP and start building in public.
What You Will Learn

    Implementing the privacy-critical core of your product first,

    Writing user-facing and technical documentation,

    Running CI/CD on your own codebase,

    Building in public and launching a product X profile.

Requirements to Pass

    Working MVP live on Preprod (verifiable address),

    Documentation (README + setup + usage),

    CI/CD pipeline running on the product repo,

    Product X profile created, linked in the README,

    Minimum 15 meaningful commits.

    Public GitHub repository with full documentation,

    Live Preprod demo link + contract address,

    CI/CD badge or workflow file with passing runs,

    Link to the product X profile,

    Demo video of the MVP,

    Minimum 15 meaningful commits.
